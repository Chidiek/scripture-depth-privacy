Privacy Policy

Scripture Depth Bible Study

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Scripture Depth Bible Study ("Scripture Depth") is a Bible reading, study, meditation, and personal reflection application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Personal Study Information

Scripture Depth allows users to create personal Bible study notes, journals, highlights, commitments, and reading progress records.

In the current version, this information is stored locally on the user's device. Scripture Depth does not currently provide account-based cloud synchronization of personal study information.

2. Information You Provide

Users may voluntarily enter reflections, prayers, study notes, and other personal content. This information is intended for the user's private study experience.

3. Internet Access

Some versions or features of Scripture Depth may retrieve Bible text or related resources from external online providers. Those providers may receive technical connection information, such as IP addresses, according to their own policies.

4. Data Sharing

Scripture Depth does not provide a feature for publicly sharing private study notes or journals with other users. We do not sell users' personal study entries to advertisers.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on the user's device until it is deleted through available app controls, app storage is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored study information. Device backup services may retain copies according to the user's device settings and backup provider's policies.

6. Security

We aim to protect users' information through appropriate application design. Users should also protect their devices using available security features. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Scripture Depth provides religious and educational content. Biblical passages may include historical accounts of violence, warfare, sexual misconduct, and other mature subjects. Parents and guardians should determine whether particular content is appropriate for children in their care.

8. Future Account Synchronization

Scripture Depth plans to introduce optional account-based synchronization, allowing users to access their notes, highlights, journals, and reading progress across their own devices.

This functionality is not currently available. Before introducing it, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant information collected, stored, processed, and protected.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will include a revised effective date.
